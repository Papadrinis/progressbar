# Claims and content rules

The channel sells personality and advice, so the rules here are about what the dog is
shown doing and saying.

## Dog welfare (default, change if you disagree)

Dachshunds are prone to back problems (IVDD), so the character is never shown jumping off
furniture, climbing stairs unaided, or being lifted without its back supported. Viewers who
own the breed notice this.
