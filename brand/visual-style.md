# Visual style

Nugget is an avatar generated from photos of Jope's real dachshund. See `dogs/nugget.md`
for his locked IDs.

- **Framing:** vertical, dog's-eye level, talking to camera by default.
- **Vibe:** a parody of the blunt, no-nonsense business mentor (Jope's reference: "muy estilo
  Alex Hormozi"). Played for laughs. Never use that person's name, logo or likeness in content.
- **Setting:** dark minimalist studio, black seamless background, hard side light. Whiteboard
  with an offer or funnel framework that has a sausage in it. Podcast mic for talk formats.
- **Palette:** black, white and mustard yellow `#F0BB2B` (from the logo). Nugget's red collar is
  the only other colour.
- **Wardrobe / props:** tight black (or grey) t-shirt with the Nuggeting logo small on the chest.
  He always keeps his red leather collar and grey collar.
- **Captions:** big, bold, condensed all-caps words, white with key words in mustard yellow.
