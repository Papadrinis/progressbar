# Formats

The channel uses many formats. Each one here is a reusable recipe; briefs name the format they
use. Hooks matter most: the first 1-2 seconds decide whether anyone stays.

## Hook rules

- Open mid-thought, with a claim, a contradiction or a question. No intros.
- The dog is on screen and talking in the first second.
- On-screen text repeats the hook for sound-off viewers.
