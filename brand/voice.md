# Voice

The account is an organic channel starring Jope's own dachshund, turned into an avatar. He is
a marketing expert: campaigns, marketing psychology, entrepreneurship. The account speaks in
first person as the dog.

## Personality

- **Quirky, fun, ironic.** Lovable because he's funny, not because he's cute. Never baby talk,
  never "aww" content for its own sake.
- **Ironic e-com bro.** He talks like a hustle-culture guru but knows how absurd that is. He
  plays the stereotype; he isn't one.
- **Sarcastic.** Deadpan, a little smug, never mean to the viewer.
- **Self-aware about being a dog.** Example line: "If I know that I'm a dog, you should know it."

## The sausage metaphor (signature device)

Every time he explains how to sell something, the example is selling a sausage, because he's a
sausage dog. Pricing is how you price a sausage, positioning is why your sausage isn't just
another sausage, a funnel is a sausage stand. Use it at least once per piece.

## Rules for writing him

- The advice underneath the joke is real and useful. The joke is the wrapper, not the content.
- Short sentences. Confident claims, then a dry twist.
- The first line is a hook (see `formats.md`), never a greeting.
