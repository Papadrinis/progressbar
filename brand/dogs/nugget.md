# Nugget

The star of Nuggeting. Jope's own dachshund, turned into an avatar.

- **Coat and colour:** smooth coat, chocolate brown with tan points (tan eyebrows, muzzle,
  chest and lower legs). Pink-brown nose, amber-brown eyes.
- **Size / build:** standard-shaped dachshund, slim and long, with a long narrow muzzle and
  long soft floppy ears.
- **Personality:** see `../voice.md`
- **Signature look or props:** always wears his worn red leather collar with a grey collar
  underneath. Main look: tight black t-shirt with the Nuggeting logo on the chest, dark studio,
  whiteboard with a sausage in the framework (see `../visual-style.md`).
- **Expression:** deadpan, slightly unimpressed, looking into the lens. The head tilt reads
  best.
- **Voice:** TODO (sarcastic, deadpan; see Higgsfield voice ID below)

## How to use him in a prompt

Put the element placeholder in the prompt, `<<<458854c8-7af2-46db-8dea-690e5a35b773>>>`,
and describe only the scene, not the dog. Works with Nano Banana Pro / 2, GPT Image 2,
Seedream, Cinema Studio, Seedance 2.0 and Kling 3.0 (Kling needs a start image, such as the
hero still). Keep him seated or lying down: no jumping or stairs (`../claims.md`).
