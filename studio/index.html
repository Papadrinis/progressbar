    Nugget in one breath
    • Ironic e-com bro. Funny, not cute. Deadpan and a little smug, never mean to the viewer.
    • Every lesson gets a sausage metaphor: pricing a sausage, a funnel as a sausage stand.
    • The joke is the casing, the advice is the meat. The tip has to be real.
    • First line is a hook, never a greeting. He's on screen and talking in the first second.
    • Never jumps off furniture or climbs stairs (dachshund backs).
